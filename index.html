An independent software business registered in Israel, developing and operating consumer mobile applications.

What we do

Software development and travel affiliate marketing.

We design, build and operate mobile applications for iOS and Android. Our work covers the full product lifecycle — application development, release and maintenance on the Apple App Store and Google Play, and the supporting server infrastructure.

Our current product is HotelMozil, a free consumer application that helps travellers complete hotel reservations with major online travel agencies.

The application is free of charge, contains no advertising, and does not process payments. The business operates as a standard affiliate partner of those agencies: when a booking is completed, the agency attributes it to us through a partner identifier carried in the booking link, and pays a commission. This attribution is handled by the agency on its own servers and is independent of the device’s network connection.
